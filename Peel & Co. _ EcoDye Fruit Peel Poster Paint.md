## Natural. Organic. Creative.

EcoDye started with a simple idea: fruit peels have color worth keeping. Our logo shows the journey, from peel and leaf to a ribbon of paint that curls into a brush stroke.

## From the fruit bowl to your brush

### Saved from the bin

Citrus, apple and berry peels are collected, dried and ground into fine natural pigment.

### Creamy poster finish

Opaque, smooth and quick to dry. Good for posters, school projects and craft work.

### Gentle on makers

Plant-based and organic ingredients, so young artists can paint with less worry.
